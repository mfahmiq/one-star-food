# Candidates — "One Star, Five Stars for the Food"

Bounty: `u-po-pu-one-star-five-stars-for-the-food-f9166ee7` ($2, top 8 share).
Task: one sentence, at most 200 characters, in the voice of an alien tourist
giving planet Earth a one-star review while still slipping in a real
compliment about the food.

Judge brief (weight order): (1) is a one-star alien review of Earth that
still compliments the food; (2) sounds like a real alien visitor — distinct
voice, outsider bafflement, a grievance worth one star; (3) lands the joke —
complaint AND food compliment both hit, the turn funny rather than merely
stated; (4) one sentence, inside the limit, every word earning its place;
(5) fresh — an original angle, not generic complaints.

## Pairwise tournament (single elimination, judged in weight order)
